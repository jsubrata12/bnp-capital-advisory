# **BRATA CAPITAL**

### **PT Brata Nusa Pratama**

## **Originating Indonesia’s Next Generation of Investable Assets**

Established in Jakarta in 1999, Brata Capital is a proprietary investment and project origination firm focused on developing high-potential opportunities across Indonesia.

We operate primarily at the pre-Final Investment Decision stage, where attractive concepts must be transformed into technically credible, commercially viable and institutionally investable projects.

Our role is simple:

**Identify opportunity. Structure it. De-risk it. Prepare it for scale.**

Brata Capital invests exclusively using its own proprietary capital and does not raise or manage public funds.

# **OUR ORIGINATION MODEL**

## **1\. Search & Identify**

We identify structural inefficiencies, policy tailwinds, industrial bottlenecks and emerging investment themes.

## **2\. Elaborate**

We develop technical, commercial and financial models, supported where required by specialist engineering, environmental and technical advisers.

## **3\. Solidify**

We address regulatory pathways, concessions, land, ESG, stakeholders, commercial counterparties and execution structure.

## **4\. Syndicate & Scale**

Once sufficiently de-risked, projects can be introduced to strategic investors, infrastructure funds, financial institutions, sovereign capital and development partners.
